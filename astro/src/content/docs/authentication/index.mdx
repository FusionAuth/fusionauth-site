FusionAuth logs users in with many methods. Start with the decision that shapes the rest of your integration: use the [hosted login pages or the Login API](/docs/concepts/hosted-login-vs-api-login). Most apps use the hosted login pages with [OAuth and OpenID Connect](/docs/authentication/oauth).

Then add the login methods your users need:

* Passwordless login with [passkeys](/docs/authentication/passkeys) or [one-time passwords](/docs/authentication/one-time-passwords).
* A second factor with [multi-factor authentication](/docs/authentication/multi-factor-authentication).
* Login through Google, Apple, an enterprise directory, or another [identity provider](/docs/authentication/identity-providers).
* [Single sign-on](/docs/authentication/sessions/single-sign-on) across your applications.

To let other applications log users in through FusionAuth, see [Host a SAML v2 Identity Provider](/docs/authentication/act-as-an-idp/host-saml-idp), and the integration guides for [OpenID Connect](/docs/authentication/act-as-an-idp/oidc) and [SAML v2](/docs/authentication/act-as-an-idp/saml).
