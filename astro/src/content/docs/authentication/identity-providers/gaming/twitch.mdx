## Create a Twitch Application

Twitch requires a verified account with two-factor authentication turned on. When you already have a Twitch app for FusionAuth, check the app against the following steps instead of creating an app.

<Steps>

1. Log in to the [Twitch Developer dashboard](https://dev.twitch.tv/).
1. Open the [Twitch application console](https://dev.twitch.tv/console/apps) and create an app.
1. Set **OAuth Redirect URLs** to the [[callback URL]] of FusionAuth, such as `https://login.piedpiper.com/oauth2/callback`. Twitch requires an `HTTPS` URL.

   ![The Twitch form to manage an application, with the OAuth redirect URL, the category, the client Id, and the client secret.](/img/docs/lifecycle/authenticate-users/identity-providers/gaming/twitch-create-app.png)

1. Select the **Category** of your application.
1. Fill out the other required fields, and click <Breadcrumb>Create</Breadcrumb>. Twitch returns you to the applications dashboard.
1. Click <Breadcrumb>Manage</Breadcrumb> on the app to see the client Id.
1. Click <Breadcrumb>New Secret</Breadcrumb> to create a client secret.
1. Copy the client Id and the client secret. The FusionAuth Identity Provider needs both values.

</Steps>
