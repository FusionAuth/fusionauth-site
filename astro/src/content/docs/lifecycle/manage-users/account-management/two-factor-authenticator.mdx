The Authenticator method is enabled by default on every tenant. To toggle please navigate to <Breadcrumb>Tenants -> Edit Tenant -> Multi-Factor</Breadcrumb>. The authenticator method is also referred to as Google Authenticator or Time-Based One-Time Password (TOTP).

## Enable MFA method on Tenant (Admin Facing)

The authenticator factor is enabled by default at the tenant level.  It can be toggled on and off as needed.

<Screenshot id="account-tenant-mfa" url="/admin/tenant/edit/30663132-6464-6665-3032-326466613934#multi-factor" alt="The tenant Multi-Factor tab with the authenticator method enabled." />

## Enable Authenticator Factor from Account Management (User Facing)

<Screenshot id="account-home" url="/account/?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The account page with the edit button and the two-factor and passkey links." />

1. Navigate back to your account page.
1. Click <Breadcrumb>Manage two-factor</Breadcrumb>
1. Click <Breadcrumb>Add two-factor</Breadcrumb>
1. There will be an option for an `Authenticator app`.

<Screenshot id="account-two-factor" url="/account/two-factor/?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The two-factor page with no methods configured." />

<Screenshot id="account-two-factor-authenticator" url="/account/two-factor/enable?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The Enable two-factor form for an authenticator app." />

Next,

1. Scan the QR code with your favorite authenticator application such as Google Authenticator or Authy.
1. (Optional) Enter a name for this method (for example, `Google Authenticator`).
1. Enter the provided code.
1. Click the <Breadcrumb>Enable</Breadcrumb> button.

## Recovery Codes (User Facing)

Now you will be presented with recovery codes. Save these in a safe space.

<Screenshot id="account-recovery-codes" url="/account/two-factor/enable?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The recovery codes shown after two-factor authentication is enabled." />

<Icon name="trophy" /> Success!

Upon the next login, you will be prompted for a code displayed by the Authenticator App in addition to your password.

## See It in Action (User Facing)

With the Authenticator method enabled, if you log out and log back in you will be presented with the following screen in addition to the typical login screen.
