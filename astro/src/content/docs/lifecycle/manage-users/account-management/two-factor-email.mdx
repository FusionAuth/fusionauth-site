Email two-factor is not enabled by default. Configure your tenant by navigating to <Breadcrumb>Tenants -> Edit Tenant -> Multi-Factor</Breadcrumb>.

## Enable MFA method on Tenant (Admin Facing)

<Screenshot id="account-tenant-mfa-email" url="/admin/tenant/edit/30663132-6464-6665-3032-326466613934#multi-factor" alt="The tenant Multi-Factor tab with the Email settings highlighted.">
  <Highlight selector="[data-hl='section-box']" />
</Screenshot>

You can enable SMS MFA on the tenant level by following these steps:

- Toggle this MFA method by clicking `enabled`
- Select a two-factor authentication template (FusionAuth ships with a default to be customized if needed)

## Enable Email Factor from Account Management (User Facing)

<Screenshot id="account-home" url="/account/?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The account page with the edit button and the two-factor and passkey links." />

1. Navigate back to your account page.
2. Click <Breadcrumb>Manage two-factor</Breadcrumb>
3. Click <Breadcrumb>Add two-factor</Breadcrumb>
4. There will be an option for Email.

<Screenshot id="account-two-factor" url="/account/two-factor/?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The two-factor page with no methods configured." />

<Screenshot id="account-two-factor-email" url="/account/two-factor/enable?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The Enable two-factor form for email with an email address entered." />

Next,

1. Enter your email address.
1. Click on `Send a one-time-code`.
1. (Optional) Enter a name for this method (for example, `Work email`).
1. Enter the provided code.
1. Click <Breadcrumb>Enable</Breadcrumb>

## Recovery Codes (User Facing)

Now you will be presented with recovery codes. Save these in a safe space.

<Screenshot id="account-recovery-codes" url="/account/two-factor/enable?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The recovery codes shown after two-factor authentication is enabled." />

<Icon name="trophy" /> Success!

Upon the next login, you will be prompted for an emailed code in addition to your password.

## See It in Action (User Facing)

With email MFA enabled, if you log out and log back in you will be presented with the following screen in addition to the typical login screen.
