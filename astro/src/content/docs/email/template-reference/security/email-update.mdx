FusionAuth sends this email when a user's email address changes. FusionAuth sends the email twice: once to the previous address and once to the new address. Assign the template in **Identity update** on the <Breadcrumb>Tenants -> Your Tenant -> Identities</Breadcrumb> tab. To override the template for one Application, set the template on the <Breadcrumb>Applications -> Your Application -> Identities</Breadcrumb> tab.
