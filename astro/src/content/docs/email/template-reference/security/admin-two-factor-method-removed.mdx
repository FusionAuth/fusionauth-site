FusionAuth sends this email when an administrator removes one of a user's two-factor methods. FusionAuth sends the email only to a verified email address. Assign the template in **Admin two-factor method removal** on the <Breadcrumb>Tenants -> Your Tenant -> Identities</Breadcrumb> tab.
