FusionAuth sends this email when a child who can't register alone asks a parent to create an account, on the hosted login pages or through the [Request Parent Approval](/docs/apis/families/request-parental-approval) API. Assign the template in **Family request template** on the <Breadcrumb>Tenants -> Your Tenant -> Family</Breadcrumb> tab.
