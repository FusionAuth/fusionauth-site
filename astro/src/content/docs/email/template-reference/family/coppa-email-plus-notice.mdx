FusionAuth sends this follow-up email after a parent grants a consent that uses Email Plus. FusionAuth sends the email at a random time within the consent's configured range. Assign the template in **Email template** under the Email Plus settings of the consent under <Breadcrumb>Settings -> Consents</Breadcrumb>. For more about consents, see the [Consents API](/docs/apis/consents).
