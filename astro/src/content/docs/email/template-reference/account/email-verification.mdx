FusionAuth sends this email to verify a user's email address: after a user registers or changes an email address, when you call the [Verify Email](/docs/apis/users/verify-email) API, and for [identity pre-verification](/docs/users/verify/identity-pre-verification-using-email). Assign the template in **Verification template** on the <Breadcrumb>Tenants -> Your Tenant -> Identities</Breadcrumb> tab. To override the template for one Application, set **Verification template** on the <Breadcrumb>Applications -> Your Application -> Identities</Breadcrumb> tab.
