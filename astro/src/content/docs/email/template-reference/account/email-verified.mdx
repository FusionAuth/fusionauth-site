FusionAuth sends this email after a user verifies an email address. Assign the template in **Verification complete template** on the <Breadcrumb>Tenants -> Your Tenant -> Identities</Breadcrumb> tab. To override the template for one Application, set the template on the <Breadcrumb>Applications -> Your Application -> Identities</Breadcrumb> tab.
