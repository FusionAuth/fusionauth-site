Features may differ between {props.migration_source_name} and FusionAuth. User data is simpler: a user who arrives with a login identifier, such as an email address or username, and a password hash can log in to FusionAuth. To learn the differences before you migrate, [download FusionAuth](/download) and build a proof of concept.
