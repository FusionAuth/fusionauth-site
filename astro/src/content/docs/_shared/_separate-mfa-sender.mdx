<Aside type="note" title="Use a separate sender for MFA">
When a user opts out of messages from a sender, such as by replying STOP to an SMS message, the messaging provider can block every later message from that sender, including MFA codes. Send MFA codes from a different sender than other messages, such as marketing messages. Depending on the provider, a separate sender is a separate phone number, messaging service, or account.
</Aside>
