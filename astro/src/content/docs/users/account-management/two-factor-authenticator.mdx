An [[authenticator application]], such as Google Authenticator or Authy, generates a [[Time-Based One-Time Password]] (TOTP) for the user to enter at login.

## Enable the Authenticator Method on the Tenant

FusionAuth enables the authenticator method on every tenant by default. To turn the method on or off, navigate to <Breadcrumb>Tenants -> Edit Tenant -> Multi-Factor</Breadcrumb> and set **Enable authenticator (TOTP) MFA**.

<Screenshot id="account-tenant-mfa" url="/admin/tenant/edit/30663132-6464-6665-3032-326466613934#multi-factor" showHash alt="The tenant Multi-Factor tab with the authenticator method enabled." />

## Add an Authenticator on the Account Pages

<Steps>

1. On the account page, click <Breadcrumb>Manage two-factor</Breadcrumb>.

   <Screenshot id="account-home" url="/account/?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The account page with the edit button and the two-factor and passkey links." />

1. Click <Breadcrumb>Add two-factor</Breadcrumb>.

   <Screenshot id="account-two-factor" url="/account/two-factor/?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The two-factor page with no methods configured." />

1. Select `Authenticator app`.

   <Screenshot id="account-two-factor-authenticator" url="/account/two-factor/enable?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The Enable two-factor form for an authenticator app." />

1. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app.
1. Optionally, enter a name for the method, such as `Google Authenticator`.
1. Enter the code from the authenticator app.
1. Click <Breadcrumb>Enable</Breadcrumb>.

</Steps>
