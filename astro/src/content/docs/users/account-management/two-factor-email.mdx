With the email method, FusionAuth emails a one-time code to the user at login.

## Enable the Email Method on the Tenant

FusionAuth doesn't enable the email method by default. To enable the email method:

<Steps>

1. Navigate to <Breadcrumb>Tenants -> Edit Tenant -> Multi-Factor</Breadcrumb>.
1. Turn on **Enable email MFA**.
1. Select a **Template**. FusionAuth ships with a default template that you can customize. An Application can override the template on its <Breadcrumb>Multi-Factor</Breadcrumb> tab.

   <Screenshot id="account-tenant-mfa-email" url="/admin/tenant/edit/30663132-6464-6665-3032-326466613934#multi-factor" showHash alt="The tenant Multi-Factor tab with the Email settings highlighted.">
     <Highlight selector="[data-hl='section-box']" />
   </Screenshot>

</Steps>

FusionAuth sends the code with the tenant's email settings. For more information, see [Configure Email](/docs/email/configure-email).

## Add Email on the Account Pages

<Steps>

1. On the account page, click <Breadcrumb>Manage two-factor</Breadcrumb>.

   <Screenshot id="account-home" url="/account/?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The account page with the edit button and the two-factor and passkey links." />

1. Click <Breadcrumb>Add two-factor</Breadcrumb>.

   <Screenshot id="account-two-factor" url="/account/two-factor/?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The two-factor page with no methods configured." />

1. Select `Email`.

   <Screenshot id="account-two-factor-email" url="/account/two-factor/enable?client_id=e9fdb985-9173-4e01-9d73-ac2d60d1dc8e" alt="The Enable two-factor form for email with an email address entered." />

1. Enter an email address.
1. Click <Breadcrumb>Send a one-time code</Breadcrumb>.
1. Optionally, enter a name for the method, such as `Work email`.
1. Enter the code from the email.
1. Click <Breadcrumb>Enable</Breadcrumb>.

</Steps>
