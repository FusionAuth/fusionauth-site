Every user account moves through the same stages:

* Users join through [registration](/docs/users/register), or arrive from another system through [provisioning](/docs/users/provision/scim) or a [migration](/docs/migrate).
* You [verify](/docs/users/verify/gate-accounts-until-user-email-verified) email addresses and phone numbers.
* Users update profile data through [self-service account management](/docs/users/account-management). Your team manages users in the Admin UI, with [search](/docs/users/search) and [user actions](/docs/users/user-actions).
* Users who forget a password reset the password through [account recovery](/docs/users/account-recovery).
* When a user leaves, you [deactivate or delete](/docs/apis/users/delete) the account.
