Authentication confirms who someone is. Authorization decides what someone can do. FusionAuth offers several options, from simple to complex:

* [Roles](/docs/concepts/types/roles) and [groups](/docs/concepts/types/groups) give users permissions in each application. Your app reads the roles from the access token.
* [OAuth scopes](/docs/authorization/oauth-scopes) limit what a client can do on behalf of a user.
* [Fine-grained authorization](/docs/authorization/fine-grained-authorization) with a service such as Permify models permissions on individual resources.
* [Token exchange](/docs/authorization/token-exchange) lets a service call another service on behalf of the original user.
* An [API gateway](/docs/authorization/api-gateways) enforces access before requests reach your services.

For the difference between authentication and authorization, see [Authentication and Authorization](/docs/concepts/authentication-authorization).
