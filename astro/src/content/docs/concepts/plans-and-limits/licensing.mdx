This page explains how to use a license with a FusionAuth instance.

For information about plans and corresponding features, see [plans and features](/docs/concepts/plans-and-limits/plans-features).

To access features that require a license, visit the account portal at [account.fusionauth.io](https://account.fusionauth.io). In the account portal, you can register for a free **Licensed Community** plan or purchase a license for a **Starter**, **Essentials**, or **Enterprise** plan. For information about pricing, see our [price calculator](/pricing) or [contact our sales team](/contact).

## Add Your License to Your Instance

Here's a brief video documenting how to find and add your license to your instance if you have a paid plan.

<YouTube id="ss2U5VKo42M" />

Once a license is purchased you must activate it.
To do so, log into your [Account](https://account.fusionauth.io/account).
Navigate to the <Breadcrumb>Plan</Breadcrumb> tab.

![View the plan tab.](/img/docs/get-started/core-concepts/plan-tab.png)

Copy the [[license key]] appropriate for your needs.

Use the "Production" license key for your production server.
This will be used to calculate your [[monthly active users]] (MAU), which may affect your monthly bill.
[Learn more about MAU here](/docs/concepts/types/users#what-makes-a-user-active).
The other license key is suitable for non-production environments, such as user acceptance testing or development.

<Aside type="note">
  You can always find your license key by logging in to your [Account](https://account.fusionauth.io/account) and then navigating to the <Breadcrumb>Plan</Breadcrumb> tab. If you do not have a license key there, you are on the Community plan. In that case, no license is required.
</Aside>

After you have your license key, log in to the Admin UI of your FusionAuth instance.
The Admin UI credentials have no connection to the credentials for your account portal.
Navigate to <Breadcrumb>Plan & Features</Breadcrumb>, enter your license key in the **License key** field, and click <Breadcrumb>Activate license</Breadcrumb>.

<Aside type="note">
To view the <Breadcrumb>Plan & Features</Breadcrumb> page, you need the `admin` or `reactor_manager` role in the FusionAuth application.
</Aside>

<Screenshot id="licensing-reactor-activate" url="/admin/reactor/" width={1300} height={700} alt="The Reactor page on an unlicensed instance, with an empty License key field and an Activate license button." />

When your instance has no outbound network access, use an air-gapped license.
Click <Breadcrumb>More options</Breadcrumb>, check **I have an air-gap license**, and paste the license text into the **License text** field.
You can find the license text on the <Breadcrumb>Plan</Breadcrumb> tab of the [account portal](https://account.fusionauth.io/account/plan).
For more information, see [Air-Gapping](/docs/deploy/production/install/air-gapping).

After you activate the license, FusionAuth connects to FusionAuth's servers to confirm the license.
Activation can take a minute or two, and you may need to refresh the page.

When activation finishes, **Current plan** shows your plan with a green checkmark.
**License type** shows whether the license is a production or non-production license.
The <Breadcrumb>Features & support</Breadcrumb> section lists each feature and whether your plan includes the feature.
For the features in each plan, see [Plans and Features](/docs/concepts/plans-and-limits/plans-features).

## License Types

There are two licenses, a production license and a non-production one. Use the production license on any system where real live users login. This will be tracked for purposes of MAU calculations, and will affect your monthly bill.

Non-production licenses are used for any other purpose: development, UAT, testing, CI/CD, etc. Any logins which occur on an instance with such a license will not be part of the MAU count.

## Regenerate Your License

You may want to regenerate your license for any number of reasons.

* It may be part of a regular secrets rotation system.
* You may have inadvertently exposed your license key.

You can do so using the [Reactor API](/docs/apis/reactor).

But you can also regenerate the license via the <Breadcrumb>Plan</Breadcrumb> tab.
To do so, log into your [Account](https://account.fusionauth.io/account). Then navigate to the <Breadcrumb>Plan</Breadcrumb> tab. Then click the <Breadcrumb>Action</Breadcrumb> button to display the dropdown.

![The license action dropdown.](/img/docs/get-started/core-concepts/license-action-dropdown.png)

After you choose which license to regenerate, you'll be prompted to confirm your choice.

![Regenerate the license key.](/img/docs/get-started/core-concepts/regenerate-license.png)

## Kickstart

You can set your license key using [[Kickstart]]. Doing so allows you to use features requiring a license in your development environment and continuous integration systems. To do this, use the non-production license key in your Kickstart files.

## Air-gapped License

You can run FusionAuth with limited or no network access with an [air-gapped license](/docs/deploy/production/install/air-gapping). For more information, [contact sales](/contact).

## Active User Count

Any user who logs in during the time period. It doesn't matter if they log in once or one thousand times. [Learn more about what counts as a login here](/docs/concepts/types/users#what-makes-a-user-active).

Other questions about licensing, MAU overages and certifications can be found in [the License FAQ](/license-faq).
