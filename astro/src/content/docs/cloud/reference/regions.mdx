## Asia/Pacific

## Europe

* France
* Germany
* Ireland
* Sweden

## Middle East

* Bahrain

## North America

* Canada
* USA

If none of our current locations meet your needs, please [contact our sales team](/contact) to discuss other options. You can also [download and self-host FusionAuth](/docs/get-started/download-and-install/docker) if you need data residency in a country not listed above and have the expertise to operate FusionAuth.
