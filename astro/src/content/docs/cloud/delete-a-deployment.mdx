You can delete a FusionAuth Cloud deployment at any time to stop billing and delete your data. For Business and High Availability deployments, FusionAuth retains a backup for a maximum of 30 days. For Basic deployments, no backups are available.

## Prerequisites

Before destroying a deployment, back up your user data and configuration. You can retrieve your configuration using the APIs.

See [Accessing User Data](/docs/cloud/reference/user-data) for more about retrieving user data and passwords.

## Delete a deployment

To delete a FusionAuth deployment:

<Steps>
1. Log into your account at [account.fusionauth.io](https://account.fusionauth.io/).
1. Navigate to the <Breadcrumb>Hosting</Breadcrumb> page. Click the **Action** button, and select the **Delete** option from the dropdown:
   ![Begin the process of destroying a deployment.](/img/docs/cloud/deployments-prepare-destroy.png)
1. Confirm your decision by typing `DELETE` in the **Confirm** box.
1. After confirmation, the deployment will transition to the **Destroying** state.
1. After the deployment is completely removed, it will have a "Destroyed" state on the <Breadcrumb>Hosting</Breadcrumb> page. At this point you will no longer be charged for this deployment.
1. If you configured [custom domains](/docs/cloud/operate/custom-domains), remove the DNS records you added.
</Steps>
